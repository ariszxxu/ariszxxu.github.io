Hi there! I'm a third-year PhD candidate (Aug. 2024 - ) at School of Computing, NUS, advised by Prof. Lin Shao. I am supported by the President's Graduate Fellowship (PGF).

I'm a visiting PhD student at UC Berkeley with Toru Lin and Prof. Jitendra Malik.

I obtained my B.Eng. (Sep. 2020 - Jun. 2024) in Robotics from Zhejiang University, where I had the privilege of working with Kechun Xu, Prof. Rong Xiong, and Prof. Yue Wang.

I study dexterous manipulation through scalable learning, transfer across robot hands, and tactile sensing.
